# Prompt Documentation

## Master prompt

Build a secure and responsive insurance CRM for SecureLife Insurance PLC with:

- a public website
- insurance plan pages
- a lead generation form
- CRM features for lead management, advisor assignment, user roles, and plan management
- a simple dashboard for management visibility

Use a clean, professional UI and make sure the public website and CRM are connected.

## Additional prompts used

1. Explain the assignment in simple terms and identify what a lead means.
2. Clarify the flow from visitor to lead to advisor to enrolled customer.
3. Clarify the role of insurance advisors and sales pipeline visibility.
4. Build the project in `D:\Projects` with a practical Next.js implementation.
5. Add public pages, a lead form, and an admin dashboard.
